# FUTURE.md

Ideas that were tempting but are **not** in `WAYFARE_PROJECT.md`. They live here until a human decides.

Adding to this file is a deliberate act of restraint. The point of the project is the sky, not the feature list.

## Park here, don’t build

- Optional git-based sync between family devices (one-way push of the Fieldbook as markdown).
- Shareable print-ready yearly scrapbook (PDF of the year’s entries plus sky log).
- Audio read-aloud versions of the Story sections, recorded by family members.
- A tiny in-app sky compass for the Moon’s horns-to-horizon trick (would need a magnetometer permission — think hard first).
- A map-the-names generator: take a local place name, pull its etymology, prompt a Fieldbook page.
- A printable monthly almanac page: moonrise/set, Orion rise times, ISS passes for the month.
- Integration with OS OpenData place names for UK families.
- A “bring this chapter into the next season” rule for chapters like Cygnus that span two seasons.

## Parked hardware ideas

- A very small e-ink device that just shows the Moon phase for today. Would break a rule (“no new screens”) but is tempting.

## Things rejected outright

- Streaks. XP bars. Levels. Badges.
- Push notifications.
- Social feed / feed of other families.
- AI chatbot / AI chapter generation.
- In-app sky map. (Link to Stellarium and be done.)
- Any kind of “share to social media” button.
